# Media in True Worship

The Media tab is the library for your own images, videos and audio. Its
side panel filters by kind: All, Videos, Images and Audio, each with an
icon.

- **Add media…** copies files into the app's data folder (JPG, PNG,
  WebP, MP4, WebM, M4V, MOV, MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, FLAC), so they stay
  available even if the originals move. In a plain browser they last
  until the page closes.
- **Images and videos:** click to preview, double-click to put it live.
  An image is fitted to the screen; a video loops with no words over it.
- **Audio:** click a track to play it, click again to stop.
- **Remove:** hover a thumbnail and press x.

Use only media you have the right to use in church. The Backgrounds tab
is separate: it sets the look behind the words, while Media is content
you show on its own.
