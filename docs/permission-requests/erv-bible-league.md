# Permission request: Easy-to-Read Version (ERV) in True Worship

**To:** Bible League International, Permissions, permissions@bibleleague.org
**From:** Toluwani Bakare, Lagos, Nigeria, mosesbakare48@gmail.com
**Date:** 26 November 2026
**Subject:** Permission to include the Easy-to-Read Version in True Worship, a free worship presentation app

Dear Bible League International,

My name is Toluwani Bakare. I am a software developer in Lagos, Nigeria, and I build True Worship, a free and open source worship presentation app for churches. The project is backed by Voice of Truth Apostolic Global Ministries (voiceoftruthonline.com), and True Worship is built under them. The app is still in development. It is a clean room alternative to paid presentation software: churches use it to show song lyrics and Bible verses on screen during services. The app is free, nothing in it is sold, and the code is open source under the GPL-3.0 licence at github.com/toluwanibakare/true-worship.

I am writing to ask for permission to include the full text of the Holy Bible, Easy-to-Read Version, as an optional downloadable pack inside the app. A church that wants it would choose to download it, and the text would then live only on that church's own computer, so their services work offline. The pack would not be bundled into the app itself, and no one would be charged for it.

I understand the ERV may be quoted up to 1,000 verses without written permission, and that a whole Bible goes beyond that, which is why I am asking first. If you grant permission, I will show your copyright notice with the text exactly as you specify, and I will follow any other conditions you set, including how the text is credited and where the pack file is hosted.

Thank you for the ERV, and for the work Bible League does in putting Bibles into the hands of churches. I would be glad to answer any questions about the app or how the text would be used.

Yours sincerely,

Toluwani Bakare
